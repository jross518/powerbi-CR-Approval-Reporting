# Executive Summary: CR Approval Reporting

## What this report shows
- Total CR volume and approval outcomes.
- Approval speed against SLA by period.
- Bottlenecks by team, approver, and request type.
- Risk concentration (high impact/high urgency CRs awaiting approval).

## Decisions enabled
- Where to allocate approver capacity.
- Which queues need escalation.
- Whether policy changes are improving cycle time.
- Which portfolio areas carry the most approval risk.

## Leadership view
- **Health indicators:** SLA attainment, median approval time, exception rate.
- **Action indicators:** aged pending approvals, rejected trends, rework trends.

## Operating cadence
- Weekly ops review for bottlenecks and SLA exceptions.
- Monthly leadership review for trend and policy outcomes.
